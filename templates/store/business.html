Distributor FAQ

Partnership FAQ

Find fast answers about qualifications, sales points calculations, single-leg support, and payment schedules.

You can apply through our Contact form by selecting B2B Partnerships. Once approved, you must purchase a qualifying combo kit containing 6 of our organic wellness juices or supplements (yielding 225 SP) to activate your account.
Every wellness product is assigned a specific SP value. The system tracks points accumulated in your Organization 1 (ORG 1) and Organization 2 (ORG 2) downlines. For matched points between both organizations, you are compensated at a rate of 1 SP = ₹2.
If your downline expands rapidly on only one side (single-leg growth), you can still claim matching commissions through our Sponsor Bonus. This pays you a 50% to 100% bonus matching the binary matching earnings of your direct referrals, regardless of your personal binary leg balance.
The weekly binary ledger closes every Saturday at midnight. Calculated matching earnings are finalized, and direct-deposit payouts are processed on the subsequent Wednesday. All transactions are subject to a statutory 5% TDS deduction under government PAN regulations.
