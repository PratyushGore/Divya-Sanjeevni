Shipping & Delivery Policy

At Divya Sanjeevni, we are committed to delivering our fresh, potent botanical formulations in perfect condition. We partner with India’s leading domestic couriers to ensure reliable logistics support.

Order Dispatch & Delivery Timelines

  • Processing Window: All orders are verified and packed within 24 to 48 hours of successful payment confirmation.
  • Domestic Shipping: Orders typically arrive within 3 to 7 business days depending on geographical location. Remote zones or rural districts may require up to 9 business days.
  • Dispatch Notifications: Once your package is handed over to our courier partners, an SMS and email notification containing the AWB tracking number will be sent.

Rates and Minimum thresholds

Standard domestic shipping is charged at a flat rate of ₹80 for all orders under ₹1,500. Orders totaling ₹1,500 or more qualify for **Free Standard Shipping** nationwide.

Damaged or Lost Shipments

In the rare event that your product bottle or container is broken during transport, please take a clear photograph of the damaged item and its packaging immediately. Send the documentation to our help desk at Divyasanjeevani@gmail.com within 24 hours of receipt. Verified transit damage will be replaced free of charge.

Data Privacy & Encryption Guidelines

We prioritize user privacy and ensure that all customer and distributor data remains fully protected. This policy explains what information we gather and how we manage it.

Information Collected

We collect personal registration details (such as full name, email, phone number, and delivery address) to verify accounts, fulfill retail purchases, and dispatch orders.

Transaction Security & Payment Gateway Compliance

  • Secure SSL Encryption: All page sessions, registrations, and transactions run over Secure Socket Layer (SSL) protocol, protecting your details from intercept.
  • Tokenized Payments: We utilize third-party PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. No credit card, debit card, or netbanking credentials are stored directly on our servers.

Partnership Data Management

To provide wholesale distribution and track referral points, your transaction details (order total, sales points) are shared securely with the **Riyansh Multitrade** back-office system. We strictly forbid the sale or sharing of user records with external advertising agencies or cold-marketing systems.

Our botanical formulations are manufactured under strict supervision, ensuring close adherence to the standards set by the **Ministry of AYUSH** (Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homoeopathy), Government of India.

Riyansh Multitrade Distributor Rules

Authorized independent distributors represent the core of the Divya Sanjeevni network. To maintain a fair and professional environment, all partners must adhere to these Distributor Rules of Conduct.

Resell Price Protections (MRP vs. DP)

Distributors purchase products at the wholesale **Distributor Price (DP)** and must market products to consumers at the official **Maximum Retail Price (MRP)**. Under-cutting the MRP on open market channels, third-party shopping sites, or bidding platforms is strictly prohibited and constitutes grounds for account termination.

Ethical Marketing & Claim Compliance

  • No Deceptive Claims: Distributors must present product benefits using official product descriptions. Do not claim that supplements can prevent, diagnose, or cure critical health issues.
  • Realistic Compensation Representation: When introducing new prospective members to Riyansh Multitrade, clearly state that earnings depend on sales volume, product kits activated, and team matching points. Do not guarantee passive income without sales activity.

Policy Infractions & Termination

Compliance audits are run periodically. If a distributor is found cross-selling, violating pricing guidelines, or distributing counterfeit items, their corporate dashboard and accumulated sales points will be suspended indefinitely.
